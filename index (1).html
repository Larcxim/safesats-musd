Why SafeSats wins

The problem: New BTC holders borrow the maximum, get liquidated in the first dip, and never return to DeFi. Existing Mezo tools are built for experts — looping vaults, escrows, pro dashboards.

Our gap: Nobody owns the first-time borrower safety niche. SafeSats is the seatbelt: traffic-light risk, a safe-borrow number (not the max), and one place to immediately earn yield on MUSD via sMUSD.

Target user: First-time BTC borrowers in emerging markets (Africa, LatAm, SE Asia) using mobile/desktop MetaMask, who need plain English — not liquidation formulas.

Roadmap: 1) SMS/Telegram liquidation alerts at 150% ICR · 2) One-click "borrow-at-250% + auto-save" flow · 3) Mainnet launch + local-language guides.
